## 🚀 Featured work

### 💊 Retrofit of a pharmaceutical blister machine (Noack DPN 760) · *Sothema, 2026*
Final-year project in a GMP plant: **5 out of 5 audited components were obsolete**, with no spare parts and frequent micro-stops.

- Replaced the B&R / Romaco / Eurotherm stack with a **three-level EtherCAT architecture** (PLC, HMI, servo drive)
- Swapped the **mechanical camshaft for a virtual E-Cam** driven by an absolute SSI encoder
- Modular **GRAFCET** logic, shift-register reject sorting, recipe/alarm/parameter screens
- Method: **DMAIC**, FMEA (RPN), I/O signal tracing, functional specification, operational qualification plan

| Metric | Result *(validated offline)* |
|---|---|
| Format change time | **30–45 min → 2–3 min** |
| E-Cam accuracy | **±1.5°** (target ±2°) |
| Restart without homing | **45 s** |
| Non-conformity detection | **100 %** |

> Commissioning on the plant floor and a **digital twin** are next.

### 🥤 PET blow-molding station modeling · *PepsiCo / Varun Beverages, 2025*
Flow modeling and optimization of a PET preform blow-molding station, with digitalization and traceability for food processing.

### 🌦️ Connected weather station 4.0
**ESP32 → MQTT → Node-RED** — a full IIoT data chain from sensor to processing.

### ☀️ Solar hybridization & energy efficiency
Hybrid solar study with **PVsyst + ETAP** to cut carbon footprint, energy use, and operating costs.

### 🧠 ATM controller on FPGA
Finite-state-machine ATM controller in **VHDL**, implemented on a Cyclone III with Quartus II and validated by simulation.
